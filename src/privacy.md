We take your privacy and the security of your personal data seriously. Currently, all user data is stored securely on our local systems. The data is only accessible to the owner of the website, and no third parties have access to it. Our storage practices ensure that your personal data is kept safe and protected from unauthorized access. We use strong security measures to protect your data, including password protection and encryption, to ensure that it remains secure. As our operations evolve, we are committed to continuously reviewing our data protection practices to keep your information safe.


## Cookies policy:

### (1) introduction

This Cookies Policy explains how 14 Red Door uses cookies and similar technologies on our website [www.14rd.com]. By using our website, you agree to our use of cookies in accordance with this policy.

### (2) what are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device to help websites function efficiently and provide information to the site owner.

### (3) types of cookies we use

* Strictly Necessary Cookies: Needed for the website to function.
* Analytics Cookies: Track user behaviour to improve the site.
* Preference Based Cookies: Track things such as language used.
* Marketing Cookies: Used for marketing and advertising purposes.

### (4) how we use cookies

We use cookies to ensure proper website functionality, analyse performance, and provide relevant marketing content to summarise.

### (5) managing cookies

You can manage your cookie preferences through your browser settings or by clicking the cookie banner on our website.

### (6) third-party cookies

Google Analytics and similar tools are in place to monitor website performance, analyse user behaviour and improve user experience. These third-party cookies collect data such as website traffic, user interactions, demographic insights, to help us understand and optimise our services.

### (7) policy updates

This policy may be updated, and if so, an email will be sent to all registered users informing them about the updates.
